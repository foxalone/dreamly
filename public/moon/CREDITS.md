# Lunar surface imagery

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. LRO / LROC / LOLA; Ernie Wright.

Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Texture: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
Downloaded: 2026-10-22.

The 2025 color map is centered on 0° longitude. It is an aesthetic visualization
texture, not a scientific measurement product. Polar areas include lower
resolution albedo data. The globe uses surface imagery, not a terrain mesh.

Local Web Mercator WebP tiles are generated by `scripts/build-moon-tiles.mjs`.
No terrestrial basemap, borders, labels or Earth distances are displayed.
